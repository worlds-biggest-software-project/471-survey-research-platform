# 471 - Survey & Research Platform

**Date:** 2026-05-02

## Overview

A survey and research platform enables organisations to design, distribute, and analyse questionnaires at scale. Core capabilities span question-type libraries, panel management for recruiting respondents, statistical analysis engines, and reporting dashboards. The market is bifurcated between lightweight tools suited to small and mid-sized teams and enterprise-grade experience-management suites used by global brands for continuous feedback programmes. AI-assisted features such as automated question suggestions, completion-rate prediction, and natural-language text analysis have become standard expectations in 2026.

## Market Landscape

The segment is dominated by a small number of established players, with Qualtrics and SurveyMonkey/Momentive holding the largest installed bases. Qualtrics—now part of SAP—has evolved into a full Experience Management (XM) suite spanning customer, employee, product, and brand research and is used by more than 13,000 organisations worldwide. SurveyMonkey is trusted by 250,000+ organisations and 40 million users, positioning itself as the accessible, fast-to-deploy alternative. Challenger platforms are carving out distinct niches: Typeform concentrates on conversational, high-completion survey experiences; QuestionPro targets research professionals with advanced methods such as MaxDiff and conjoint analysis; Alchemer (formerly SurveyGizmo) serves enterprises that need deep workflow integration; and Formbricks offers a self-hostable open-source alternative aimed at privacy-conscious buyers.

## Key Features / Tools

1. **Drag-and-drop survey builder** — multi-format question types (multiple choice, ranking, matrix, NPS, open text), branching logic, and skip patterns
2. **AI question assistant** — suggests questions based on research objective, flags ambiguous wording, and predicts completion rates before launch
3. **Panel management** — audience sourcing, screening quotas, incentive distribution, and panel health monitoring
4. **Multi-channel distribution** — email, SMS, embedded web, QR code, in-product intercepts, and API triggers
5. **Real-time analytics dashboard** — cross-tabulation, sentiment analysis, word clouds, and trend comparisons
6. **Statistical analysis engine** — significance testing, regression, conjoint analysis (MaxDiff), and weighting for representative samples
7. **Report builder** — branded PDF/PPT exports, shareable live links, and scheduled digest emails
8. **Integration hub** — connectors to Salesforce, HubSpot, Slack, Tableau, and 200+ other tools via Zapier/native APIs
9. **Multilingual surveys** — machine-translation assistance, right-to-left layout support, and locale-specific formatting
10. **Role-based access and audit trail** — team workspaces, response anonymisation controls, and GDPR/CCPA compliance tooling

## Competitive Differentiation Opportunities

- Combining panel recruitment, survey design, and advanced statistical analysis (conjoint, discrete choice) in a single mid-market product at a price point below Qualtrics
- First-class AI text analysis that surfaces themes and sentiment across open-ended responses without requiring a data science team
- Modular pricing that charges per response rather than per seat, reducing cost barriers for project-based researchers
- Built-in experiment design (A/B test and control group management) to bridge the gap between survey feedback and causal inference
